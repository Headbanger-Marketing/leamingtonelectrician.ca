Lighting installation in Leamington.

Plan the light and the work that makes it possible

A lighting project may involve fixture replacement, new wiring points or a different control layout. The contractor needs to assess existing wiring, mounting support, equipment suitability and access. Fixture appearance alone does not establish compatibility with a dimmer or the suitability of a location. Share product information and the intended room use before installation is priced.

Prepare the scope for your Leamington property

Sketch the room and mark each fixture position, noting which are near the tub, shower or sink, and photograph any fixture you plan to replace. Collect the instruction sheets for fixtures already ordered, with the date they arrive.

Ask the contractor whether each location needs equipment rated for damp or wet exposure, and whether the existing wiring points can take the new fixtures. Ask what happens to the order if the assessment changes the choice.

What should the assessment establish?

Exposure to splashing or steam is noted for each fixture position in a Leamington bathroom or other damp location, with the rating that position calls for. The mounting surface and ceiling space are each marked as suiting the chosen fixtures or not. Any fan circuit is described in relation to the lighting. If lights outside the room, in a hallway or attached garage for example, share its circuit, they are named. Any ceiling or wall repair is listed, with who finishes it before a closing or move-in.

Ontario electrical safety

ESA says almost all electrical work needs a notification of work. If you hire someone for home electrical work, use a Licensed Electrical Contractor. Agree who files the notification and request the Certificate of Acceptance after ESA review.

Read the current ESA notification and inspection guidance, contractor hiring guidance. You can verify an electrical contractor with ESA before agreeing to work.

Questions before you agree to work

Can I buy bathroom fixtures before the contractor has seen the room?

It is safer to wait for the assessment, because a fixture that looks suitable may not be rated for the spot where it would hang. If an order is already placed, share the instruction sheet first.

Does a dimmer or a different switch change the bathroom scope?

It can, because the control has to suit the fixtures and the location. Mention any dimming preference when you send the enquiry, and ask the contractor to name the control in the written scope.
