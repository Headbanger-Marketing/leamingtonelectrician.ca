Renovation wiring in Leamington.

Electrical decisions belong early in the renovation

A renovation changes how the space will be used and what equipment it must serve. Provide the drawings and appliance specifications before the electrical scope is finalized. A Licensed Electrical Contractor can assess retained wiring, new circuits and the proposed loads. Municipal building permissions and ESA electrical notification are separate matters, and the project may require both.

Prepare the scope for your Leamington property

List each trade you have hired, what each needs from the electrical work and the order you expect them to arrive. Gather the drawings, appliance sheets and the finish date that other agreements already depend on.

Ask the contractor at which stages the electrical work must happen, such as before walls close, and what the other trades must leave open. Ask who is responsible for the ESA notification and any building permit.

What should the assessment establish?

Where does the electrical work fall in a renovation that a Leamington owner manages with separately hired trades? The scope places it in the sequence and marks which steps must be finished before the next trade starts. How much of the existing wiring stays? Wiring kept and wiring replaced appear as separate items, and whatever remains unknown until walls or ceilings are opened is named. Each appliance has its circuits and equipment written out from the specification sheets you supplied. Where no general contractor coordinates the trades, the quote states who patches openings and how access is kept for any inspection.

Ontario electrical safety

ESA says almost all electrical work needs a notification of work. If you hire someone for home electrical work, use a Licensed Electrical Contractor. Agree who files the notification and request the Certificate of Acceptance after ESA review.

Read the current ESA notification and inspection guidance, contractor hiring guidance. You can verify an electrical contractor with ESA before agreeing to work.

Questions before you agree to work

Who coordinates the electrical work when I hire each trade myself?

You do, unless a written agreement says otherwise. Give the contractor the names of the other trades and ask which of their steps must come before or after the electrical work.

What happens if a trade covers the walls before the electrical review?

Closed walls can hide work that has not been reviewed, and opening them again is a separate step. Ask the contractor which stages need review before covering, and confirm the order in writing.
